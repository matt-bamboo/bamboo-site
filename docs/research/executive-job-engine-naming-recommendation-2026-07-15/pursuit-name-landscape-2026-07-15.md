# Pursuit Name and Brand Landscape

**Snapshot:** 2026-07-15

**Purpose:** A focused answer to where **Pursuit** is being used now, what the word already means in the market, which active brand sites are worth studying, and what that changes for the Executive Job Engine naming recommendation.

**Scope:** Research and proposal only. No portal, code, repository, project, domain, account, handle, deployment, or public property was renamed, purchased, claimed, or changed. The internal portal remains **app.bamboo.holdings**.

**Research status:** **Preliminary public research, not legal clearance.** Search results, registry data, domains, products, and social profiles change. Domain non-resolution is not availability. Only qualified counsel can assess registrability and use risk.

## Current answer

**Pursuit is an excellent idea-word and a weak ownable master brand for this product.** It conveys agency, motion, discernment, ambition, and a serious objective. Those are all right. The problem is not meaning; it is crowding in the exact commercial neighborhood.

Three findings change the earlier recommendation materially:

1. **Pursuit already has a major active career identity.** [Pursuit.org](https://www.pursuit.org/) trains adults for technology and AI careers, works with employers, helps secure job opportunities, and provides long-term career support.
2. **The exact word mark PURSUIT has a registered public signal in directly relevant services.** The public record for US serial **88187762**, registration **6019883**, lists employment counseling and recruiting plus career training, coaching, and professional support in Classes 35 and 41. [Public trademark record](https://trademarks.justia.com/881/87/pursuit-88187762.html)
3. **PursuitRoom is a very close product analogue.** It sources roles, scores fit against preferences and a resume, creates tailored application materials, manages a pipeline, supports executives and stealth searches, and keeps application control with the candidate. [PursuitRoom](https://www.pursuitroom.com/)

### Recommendation update

| Use of the word | Semantic fit | Practical ownability | Current recommendation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Pursuit** as the public master brand | 9/10 | 2/10 | **Do not advance** |
| **Proof & Pursuit** as the public master brand | 8/10 | 4/10 | **Hold; likely reopen naming unless counsel sees a viable path** |
| **Pursuit** as a public module name | 8/10 | 5/10 | Use only after counsel reviews the proposed architecture |
| “pursuit” as ordinary workflow language | 8/10 | N/A | Retain as useful product vocabulary, not a badge |

The earlier exact-phrase search for **Proof & Pursuit** did not surface a matching active company. That is still useful, but it is no longer enough. The USPTO explains that marks do not need to be identical to conflict; similarity can arise from sound, appearance, meaning, or commercial impression. [USPTO likelihood-of-confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion)

## 2. Active sites worth looking at

These are the strongest references if the goal is to understand how a Pursuit brand can feel. They are references for tone and architecture, not designs or language to copy.

### Best overall brand reference: Pursuit.com

[Pursuit](https://www.pursuit.com/) is a global attractions and hospitality brand built around iconic places, elevated experiences, and “explore with wonder.” Its system is broad enough to endorse multiple destinations and experience brands, and the name feels confident without needing a technical explanation. The company is also large and visible; its investor site says the stock trades on the NYSE as PRSU. [About the brand](https://www.pursuit.com/about) · [Investor FAQ](https://investors.pursuit.com/resources/investor-faqs/default.aspx)

**What is useful:** restrained master-brand presence, evocative imagery, a clear emotional promise, and a portfolio architecture.

**What not to import:** adventure language would pull the career system toward lifestyle aspiration and away from evidence, judgment, and privacy.

### Best disciplined verbal system: Pursuit Boats

[Pursuit Boats](https://www.pursuitboats.com/) uses a compact, high-confidence vocabulary: “PURE. PURSUIT.”, “unrelenting performance,” “built with integrity,” and “ruggedly refined.” It makes an ordinary word feel proprietary through consistency and product proof.

**What is useful:** precision, calm confidence, integrity, and a tight phrase system.

**What not to import:** macho performance language or relentless-hustle cues would be wrong for a human, approval-gated career system.

### Best institutional-human balance: Pursuit Lending

[Pursuit Lending](https://pursuitlending.com/) leads with “Ambition, meet funding” and combines an aspirational name with sober explanations of process, products, support, and outcomes.

**What is useful:** aspiration backed immediately by practical help and institutional credibility.

**What not to import:** the “goal getter” and entrepreneurial-growth language can drift motivational if copied into an executive career context.

### Best precision/continuous-improvement expression: Pursuit Aerospace

[Pursuit Aerospace](https://pursuitaero.com/) pairs the name with complex aircraft-engine manufacturing and the line “Always Pursuing Better.”

**What is useful:** technical seriousness, quality, process, and continuous improvement.

**What not to import:** industrial coldness. The Job Engine must remain candidate-owned and human.

### Best modern software expression: Pursuit.us

[Pursuit](https://www.pursuit.us/) is a current revenue-intelligence platform for state and local government sales teams. It uses modern product language around data infrastructure, workspace, AI workflows, opportunities, and pipeline.

**What is useful:** a crisp software master brand with extensible product modules.

**What not to import:** “win,” pipeline, revenue, and opportunity capture reinforce the recruiter/GovCon/sales meaning of pursuit.

## 3. The direct collision set

### Career and training

- [Pursuit.org](https://www.pursuit.org/) has operated in technology-career transformation for roughly a decade and now emphasizes AI-native training, employer partnerships, job opportunities, and long-term career support.
- The [Pursuit LinkedIn presence](https://www.linkedin.com/school/joinpursuit/) is substantial and explicitly describes training and placing technology professionals.
- A separate [Pursuit veteran tech-sales program](https://www.linkedin.com/company/pursuitintech) describes interview positioning, strategic career planning, job entry, AI-driven practice, and long-term professional development.
- The Google Play app [Pursue: Your Career Guide](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pursueit.pursue) is not the exact word, but it adds a close phonetic and semantic career use.

### Candidate-side job technology

- [PursuitRoom](https://www.pursuitroom.com/) is the most important product collision. Its current site promises open-market sourcing, role scoring, resume fit, compensation and location preferences, tailored resumes and cover letters, a job pipeline, private executive searches, and no auto-apply.

This does not mean the products are identical. The Executive Job Engine is deeper on evidence provenance, full-posting preservation, Career Canon, decision support, and approval architecture. It does mean that a new Pursuit-branded executive job system would enter a search result and buyer conversation already occupied by an unusually similar product.

### Recruiting and executive search

- [Pursuit Sales Solutions](https://www.pursuitsalessolutions.com/) is an active sales and marketing recruiting firm.
- [Pursuit Sourcing](https://www.pursuitsourcing.com/) offers executive search, retained sourcing, recruitment-process outsourcing, and market intelligence.
- [Pursuit Executive](https://www.pursuitgroup.co.uk/executive-employers/) recruits chief executives, managing directors, and other senior leaders.
- [The Pursuit Agency](https://pursuitagency.com/services/) offers recruitment, executive search, staffing, and background checks.
- LinkedIn also surfaces active companies such as [Pursuit Talent Advisory](https://www.linkedin.com/company/pursuit-talent-advisory) and [Pursuit Resources Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/pursuitgroup/) in staffing and recruiting.

The volume matters more than any single firm: **Pursuit naturally reads as what a recruiter does**, not only what a candidate chooses.

## 5. Preliminary trademark signal

The public trademark record for **PURSUIT**, serial **88187762**, registration **6019883**, currently displays “Registered” and lists:

- employment counseling and recruiting;
- identifying and recruiting candidates for computer-programming training;
- career training and support;
- career coaching and individualized guidance;
- professional skills and interview preparation.

Source: [public record mirror](https://trademarks.justia.com/881/87/pursuit-88187762.html). The USPTO's direct TSDR API did not return case data without an API key during this screen, so this packet does not pretend to be a complete official docket review.

This is not a legal conclusion that **Proof & Pursuit** cannot be used. It is a strong reason to stop treating the name as a clean winner. Counsel should evaluate:

1. whether **Pursuit** is a dominant element of **Proof & Pursuit**;
2. relatedness between candidate-owned career software and the registered career/recruiting/training services;
3. common-law use by Pursuit.org, PursuitRoom, Pursuit.vet, and the recruiting firms;
4. both registrability and actual use risk;
5. whether branded modules or an endorsed architecture change the analysis.

## 6. What to do with the word

### Keep the concept

The product legitimately helps a person decide **what to pursue**, preserve a **pursuit record**, and manage the cost and next action of a **pursuit**. That vocabulary is useful and intuitive. It can remain in descriptive copy and internal workflow language.

### Do not make it carry the brand without clearance

I would not adopt **Pursuit**, **Bamboo Pursuit**, **PursuitOS**, **Pursuit Desk**, or **Proof & Pursuit** as the public identity on the present evidence. Adding Bamboo does not solve the career-category problem and introduces BambooHR confusion. Adding a generic product suffix does not create much distance.

### Reopen the naming territory, not the product thesis

The right territory is still **evidence plus deliberate movement**: a private system that knows what is true, judges what is worth doing, and helps the executive act without surrendering control. The next naming round should preserve that meaning while excluding the literal root **Pursuit** until counsel says otherwise.

## 7. Exact next validation step

Send counsel this bounded question before any domain, handle, design, or naming action:

> Please provide a written United States knockout for **Proof & Pursuit**, **Proof and Pursuit**, and **Proof Pursuit** for candidate-controlled career intelligence software and related advisory services. Please address US registration 6019883 / serial 88187762; Pursuit.org; PursuitRoom; Pursuit.vet; Pursuit Sales Solutions; Pursuit Sourcing; and other relevant recruiting, career, coaching, software, and common-law uses. Assess both registrability and use risk, including whether “Pursuit” would be treated as a dominant element. No filing is authorized.

Unless that answer is unusually favorable, run a focused replacement round in the same semantic territory and screen root words before polishing compounds.

## Bottom line

**Pursuit helps the story and hurts the name.** It supplies exactly the right emotional energy—agency, direction, seriousness, and movement—but it is already a career brand, a registered career-services mark, a close job-search product root, a recruiting name, an executive-search name, and an opportunity-software name. The nicest executions prove the word can feel premium; the closest executions show that Matthew is unlikely to own what it means.

The current decision should therefore be:

> **Retain “pursuit” as product language. Put Proof & Pursuit on hold. Reopen the commercial master-brand search.**
