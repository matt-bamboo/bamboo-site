## 1. Product and category definition

### Product truth

The product is not a resume builder, an ATS optimizer, an auto-apply bot, a recruiter database, or a motivational career coach. Current product evidence supports this definition:

> **A private, candidate-controlled executive career intelligence system that preserves role truth and career truth, evaluates high-stakes opportunities, creates evidence-grounded materials, and keeps every external action behind human approval.**

Its durable jobs are:

1. Discover roles and preserve the official source and full posting.
2. Separate opportunity quality from qualification evidence.
3. Reason about compensation, scope, location, relocation, upside, and pursuit cost.
4. Maintain an approved career evidence record rather than infer claims from a resume alone.
5. Build role-specific materials whose factual clauses resolve to approved evidence.
6. Track pursuits, next actions, versions, decisions, feedback, and outcomes.
7. Learn from human review without silently turning feedback into fact.
8. Prepare inside the system while leaving employer submission, email, messaging, and other external actions under human control.

The current product review names the commercial wedge **candidate-controlled executive evidence and decision infrastructure** and recommends the customer-facing category **private executive career intelligence** or **private executive opportunity system**, not “all-in-one AI job search.” See the [current code-to-market review](/Users/matthewgrossman/Documents/GitHub/bamboo-site/docs/research/executive-job-engine-code-to-market-plan-2026-07-15/report.md:10), [role-first product reset](/Users/matthewgrossman/Documents/GitHub/bamboo-private-apps/docs/JOB_ENGINE_PRODUCT_RESET_PLAN_20260713.md:59), [qualification evidence model](/Users/matthewgrossman/Documents/GitHub/bamboo-private-apps/docs/QUALIFICATION_EVIDENCE_MODEL_20260713.md:5), and [compensation search model](/Users/matthewgrossman/Documents/GitHub/bamboo-private-apps/docs/COMPENSATION_SEARCH_MODEL.md:39).

No private candidate facts, employers, compensation preferences, contacts, application details, or generated materials are reproduced in this report.

### Recommended category language

Use these in descending order of preference:

1. **Private executive career system** — clearest customer-facing descriptor.
2. **Candidate-owned career intelligence** — strongest differentiated category phrase.
3. **Executive evidence and decision system** — precise for partners and investors.
4. **Private executive opportunity OS** — useful in technical or product contexts, but more jargon-heavy.

Do not lead with:

- AI job search
- resume platform
- job tracker
- ATS optimizer
- career coach
- talent intelligence
- candidate relationship management
- applicant tracking

The last three create employer-side, staffing, or HR-system confusion.

### Hard gates

A candidate fails regardless of score when:

- an active career, hiring, staffing, evidence, or decision product uses the same or a confusingly close name;
- it depends on Bamboo as the dominant public word before BambooHR risk is cleared;
- it traps the product in resumes, applications, tracking, or AI;
- it implies automatic applying, guaranteed placement, recruiting representation, or employer-side control;
- it sounds cheap, desperate, motivational, or like an undifferentiated coaching practice;
- it requires a pronunciation lesson every time;
- a likely customer could reasonably think the product is a BambooHR module or integration.

## 4. Preliminary screening method and limitations

### Surfaces checked

The July 15 screen used, where practical:

- exact-name and close-variant web searches;
- direct company sites and LinkedIn company search;
- public trademark-signal searches plus the [USPTO federal-search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching) and [likelihood-of-confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion);
- registry RDAP and public WHOIS responses for selected domain routes;
- GitHub repository and account searches;
- Product Hunt search and search-engine indexing;
- Apple App Store search results and Google Play search pages;
- direct GitHub, LinkedIn, X, and Instagram handle paths.

The USPTO explains that marks can conflict through similar sound, appearance, meaning, or commercial impression when the goods or services are related, and that a comprehensive search should include federal, state, and internet sources. That has **not** been completed here. [USPTO search rationale](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/basics/why-search-similar-trademarks)

### What a “no result” means

“No exact result surfaced” does not mean available, registrable, safe, or unowned. It can mean:

- an unindexed or private product exists;
- a state, foreign, pending, or common-law mark was missed;
- a marketplace or social profile requires authentication;
- the name exists with a different spelling or related commercial impression;
- registry data was rate-limited or stale;
- a domain is reserved, premium, blocked, or available only through a broker.

No domain or handle is described as available in this report. “No RDAP object returned” is only the registry response observed at that moment.

## 6. Three finalists

### Finalist 1 — Recommended winner: Proof & Pursuit

#### Why it wins

Proof & Pursuit makes a useful promise without overclaiming. **Proof** holds the Career Canon, full-posting source, admissible claims, version history, and approval gates. **Pursuit** holds discovery, opportunity decisions, outreach preparation, applications, interviews, and feedback. Neither word traps the system in resumes or job tracking.

It also creates a disciplined behavior:

> Establish what is true. Decide what is worth pursuing. Prepare only what the evidence can support.

The name is not obviously AI, does not sound like an ATS, and is broad enough for portfolio careers, board roles, advisory work, founder transitions, internal executive moves, and future career capital management.

#### One-line positioning

> **A private executive career system that turns verified experience and source-backed opportunities into better pursuit decisions.**

#### Tagline options

1. **Proof before pursuit.**
2. **Evidence for the next move.**
3. **Decide with evidence. Move with confidence.**
4. **Better opportunities. Defensible claims.**
5. **Your career, grounded.**

#### Module architecture

Use functional modules rather than clever sub-brands:

| Module | Product job |
|---|---|
| **Scout** | Discover roles, reconcile identity, and preserve official sources. |
| **Briefs** | Store full postings, changes, source confidence, and structured Job Briefs. |
| **Decisions** | Separate opportunity, qualification, compensation, location, and pursuit-path judgment. |
| **Record** | Maintain approved career facts, evidence, wording constraints, and prohibited claims. This is the external-friendly name for the internal Career Canon. |
| **Studio** | Build resumes, outreach, interview preparation, and role-specific materials. |
| **Pipeline** | Track applications, relationships, stages, next actions, and exact submitted versions. |
| **Review** | Capture feedback, outcomes, rules, model cost, and human approvals. |

The initial lockup can read:

> **Proof & Pursuit**<br>
> Private executive career system<br>
> A Bamboo Holdings product

#### Plausible domain routes

Public registry checks returned no object for:

- proofandpursuit.com
- proofandpursuit.ai
- getproofandpursuit.com

The shorter proofpursuit.com is registered. These are observations, not availability claims. The internal portal should remain app.bamboo.holdings until a later, separately approved migration decision.

Do not commission visual identity, reserve a domain, or use the mark publicly until the written answer is received.

### Finalist 2 — Safer backup: Career Charter

#### Why it is safer

Career Charter is safer in comprehension, not necessarily in legal ownability. It immediately feels serious, human, and candidate-owned. A charter implies rights, standards, scope, and rules: all good metaphors for approved claims, search criteria, compensation thresholds, location constraints, and human approval gates.

## 7. Blunt Bamboo recommendation

### Bamboo helps

- Matthew genuinely likes it, which matters for a private system he will use repeatedly.
- It already has internal continuity through Bamboo Holdings and app.bamboo.holdings.
- The plant supports credible metaphors: durable structure, segmented knowledge, patient compounding, flexibility, and growth.
- It can work as parentage: “A Bamboo Holdings product.”
- It can inform restrained identity details without becoming the spoken product name.

### Bamboo hurts

BambooHR is not a remote naming coincidence. Its official applicant-tracking API covers jobs, applications, statuses, hiring leads, and candidate creation. [BambooHR applicant-tracking documentation](https://documentation.bamboohr.com/reference/applicant-tracking) BambooHR's own terms identify BambooHR as a registered trademark, while the public registration record lists SaaS for employee records, applicant tracking, onboarding, workflow, payroll, and performance management. [BambooHR terms](https://www.bamboohr.com/legal/terms-of-service) [BAMBOOHR registration signal](https://trademarks.justia.com/878/43/bamboohr-87843819.html)

The proposed product is candidate-side, while BambooHR is employer-side. That distinction helps positioning but does not erase related-service or commercial-impression risk. The USPTO specifically warns that marks need not be identical and that related goods or services, similar sound, appearance, meaning, or overall impression can be enough for confusion. [USPTO likelihood-of-confusion guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion)

The practical problem arrives before a courtroom:

- A buyer may assume Bamboo CareerOS is a BambooHR product.
- A partner may describe it as “the Bamboo career thing,” collapsing the distinction.
- Search results and app-store results will be dominated by BambooHR.
- Press, recruiters, and HR leaders may place it on the employer side.
- A bamboo leaf, green HR aesthetic, or “people platform” language would intensify the association.

### Blunt verdict

> **Bamboo helps the ownership story and hurts the commercial master brand.**

Use it in this order:

1. **Best:** Proof & Pursuit, with “A Bamboo Holdings product” in footer, about, legal, or investor contexts.
2. **Acceptable after counsel:** “by Bamboo Holdings” in a secondary lockup.
3. **Useful internally:** app.bamboo.holdings and private module architecture.
4. **Avoid:** Bamboo as the spoken product name or the dominant first word in career, talent, hiring, applicant, people, HR, or workforce software.

## 10. Unresolved risks

1. **No legal clearance.** The screen did not exhaust federal, state, common-law, international, phonetic, design-mark, or related-service conflicts.
2. **Proof & Pursuit comprehension.** The name may be read as consulting, publishing, litigation, government contracting, or sales pursuit without the descriptor.
3. **Ampersand consistency.** Spoken, typed, domain, handle, app-store, and legal forms need a single rule: display “&”; write “and” in URLs and handles.
4. **Career Charter ownability.** It may be too descriptive and too widely used to function as a strong exclusive mark.
5. **Bamboo parent endorsement.** Even secondary use may warrant counsel review because the product operates near applicant tracking and candidate records.
6. **Dynamic digital state.** Domains, company pages, stores, repositories, and handles can change immediately after this report.
7. **Commercial readiness.** Naming does not change the existing product-readiness gates. The current private system remains Matthew-specific and is not proof of a tenant-ready commercial product.
8. **International expansion.** Meanings, pronunciations, and marks outside the United States were not systematically screened.
9. **Service expansion.** Adding human coaching, placement, recruiting, or search services changes both perception and the relevant legal classes.
