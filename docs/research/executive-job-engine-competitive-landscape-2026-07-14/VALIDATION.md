# Validation report

## Overall assessment: Ready to share with caveats

The report answers the requested competitive and commercial questions, the structured artifact validates successfully, and the quantitative coverage counts reconcile. The remaining limitations are inherent to public-source research and are disclosed in the report.

## Checks completed

- 77 unique competitor or adjacency rows; every required comparison field is populated.
- 15 closest-threat rows across all 15 requested capability dimensions.
- Nine taxonomy counts sum to 77; the category chart is explicitly labeled as research coverage, not market share.
- 240 unique first-party URLs indexed; every competitor has a primary URL, supporting source trail, confidence label and July 14, 2026 access date.
- Public pricing is labeled unavailable, inconsistent, approximate or account-gated where current list price could not be verified.
- Vendor performance and scale statements are treated as vendor claims, not independent evidence.
- Pricing recommendations, roadmap thresholds, conversion gates and margin gates are explicitly labeled as hypotheses.
- Legal/regulatory dates were checked for the EU AI Act, New York City Local Law 144, California's April 1, 2026 effective date and Colorado's January 1, 2027 effective date.
- The Data Analytics artifact validator passed with snapshot status `ready`, seven bounded datasets, one native chart and five tables.
- No accounts, purchases, vendor contact, product code, Supabase, DNS or employer-facing systems were used or changed.

## Material caveats

1. Public web evidence is not equivalent to hands-on product testing; gated features may differ.
2. Prices can vary by geography, promotion, taxes, account state or checkout timing.
3. The sample is broad but is not a census and does not establish market share.
4. No independent TAM, willingness-to-pay, conversion, retention, CAC or unit-economics data exists yet.
5. Legal and privacy analysis is product-risk guidance, not legal advice.
6. “No public evidence found” is not proof a feature does not exist.

## Decision implication

The research supports a paid wedge-validation program, not a full-scale category launch. Commercial confidence should remain conditional until paid cohorts prove premium willingness to pay, factual quality, workflow adoption, advisor leverage and sustainable support/model economics.
